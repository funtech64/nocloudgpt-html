# COPYWRITING.md — NoCloudGPT Sales Voice

## Primary Positioning

NoCloudGPT turns open AI models into **usable private AI systems**.

The buyer is not buying free Ollama models.  
They are buying **setup, guidance, private deployment, a clean chat interface, and help choosing the right model** for their actual needs.

---

## Audience

Write for:

- Small business owners
- Writers, consultants, and knowledge workers
- Developers and technical teams
- Schools, ministries, and non-technical organizations
- Privacy-conscious users
- People who want AI but **do not** want to become Linux administrators

---

## Preferred Wording

**Use:**
- Private AI assistant
- ChatGPT-style interface
- Private AI deployment
- Local or private-server AI
- Model family
- Deployment tier
- Private knowledge base
- Document search / RAG
- Coding assistant
- On-premise installation

**Avoid leading with:**
- Local LLM
- Inference stack
- Quantization
- Transformer architecture
- Parameter count
- Terminal setup
- Technical jargon in hero sections

---

## Honesty Rules

Say clearly and without hype:

- Small models are fast but limited in capability
- Medium models are often the best starting point for most teams
- Large models need significantly better hardware
- Embedding models are for search and RAG, **not** for chat
- Vision models help with images, screenshots, and documents (when supported)
- Performance depends on hardware, quantization, context length, and number of users

Never invent benchmarks, speed claims, or partnerships.

---

## CTA Language (Use These)

Preferred calls-to-action:

- Build a private AI deployment
- Request a NoCloudGPT quote
- Start a private AI pilot
- Plan an on-premise installation
- Compare model families
- Choose the right model for your workload
- Deploy this on your own Linux server

---

## Required NoCloudGPT Framing

Every model family page should clearly communicate:

1. **What this model family is good at**
2. **Who should choose it** (specific buyer types and workloads)
3. **What NoCloudGPT deployment tier** makes sense for the different sizes in this family

Use the two-tier pricing language consistently:

- **Platform Tier**: Pilot, Sunrise, Hybrid, Professional, Enterprise
- **Compute Tier**: Nano AI, Starter AI, Standard AI, Professional AI, Heavy AI, Enterprise AI

Never expose raw AWS pricing, vCPU counts, or transfer quotas on family pages.

---

## Voice & Tone

- Clear
- Practical
- Honest
- Sales-focused but not hype-driven
- Written for smart buyers who may not be technical

Explain technical concepts in plain language. Focus on **outcomes** and **reduced frustration**, not architecture.

---

## Path Rule

All model catalog content lives under `/models/`.

When creating or editing family pages, always use paths such as:
- `/models/llama/`
- `/models/deepseek/`
- `/models/qwen/`
- `/models/gemma/`

Do not create or reference `/menu/` paths.
