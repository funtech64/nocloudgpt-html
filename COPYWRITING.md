COPYWRITING.md — NoCloudGPT Sales Voice

Primary positioning

NoCloudGPT turns open AI models into usable private AI systems.

The buyer is not buying free Ollama models. They are buying setup, guidance, private deployment, a chat interface, and help choosing the right model.

Audience

Write for:

* small business owners
* writers
* consultants
* developers
* schools/ministry teams
* privacy-conscious users
* people who want AI but do not want Linux complexity

Preferred wording

Use:

* private AI assistant
* ChatGPT-style interface
* private AI deployment
* local or private-server AI
* model family
* deployment tier
* private knowledge base
* document search / RAG
* coding assistant

Avoid leading with:

* local LLM
* inference stack
* quantization
* transformer architecture
* parameter count
* terminal setup

Honesty rules

Say clearly:

* small models are fast but limited
* medium models are often the best starting point
* large models need better hardware
* embedding models are for search/RAG, not chat
* vision models help with images/screenshots/documents when supported
* performance depends on hardware, quantization, context length, and active users

CTA language

Use:

* Build a private AI deployment
* Request a NoCloudGPT quote
* Start a private AI pilot
* Plan an on-premise installation
* Compare model families
* Choose the right model for your workload
