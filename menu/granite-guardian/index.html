🔍 What Is Granite Guardian?

Granite Guardian is IBM Research's dedicated AI risk detection model family, purpose-built to evaluate both the inputs and outputs of large language models for harmful, biased, or policy-violating content. Unlike simple content filters, Granite Guardian produces structured risk scores across named harm categories — giving compliance teams, platform engineers, and AI operators the granularity they need for defensible policy enforcement.

The model family spans 2B and 8B parameter sizes, enabling deployment across resource-constrained cloud instances and dedicated GPU servers. NoCloudGPT deploys Granite Guardian via Ollama on private infrastructure — your prompts and responses never leave your network.

🔄 Pipeline Integration

Granite Guardian is designed for inline bidirectional deployment — screening both the prompt entering your LLM and the response it generates before delivery to end users.

User Input Received

Input Screening (Guardian)

3
Threshold Decision
Your orchestration layer routes: PASS → primary LLM, FLAG → human review queue, BLOCK → policy refusal with logged audit event

Clean prompts proceed to your primary model (any Ollama-served LLM or external endpoint)

Full risk score vector, timestamps, model versions, and disposition logged to your SIEM or compliance store

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Granite Guardian?
Granite Guardian is IBM's open-source risk detection model family designed to identify harmful, biased, or policy-violating content in both prompts and AI-generated responses. It provides structured risk scores across multiple harm categories.
How does Granite Guardian differ from other safety models?
Granite Guardian outputs structured risk scores with category labels (violence, hate, PII exposure, prompt injection, etc.) rather than simple pass/fail verdicts, enabling fine-grained policy enforcement and audit logging.
What hardware does Granite Guardian require?
The 2B variant runs on AWS Lightsail 4GB RAM instances. The 8B variant performs optimally on 8GB RAM or entry-level GPU. Both are deployable via Ollama with zero cloud egress.
Can Granite Guardian run inline in an AI pipeline?
Yes. Granite Guardian is designed for bidirectional pipeline integration — screening inputs before they reach your primary LLM and evaluating outputs before delivery, adding minimal latency while maintaining compliance audit trails.
