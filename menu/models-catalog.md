# NoCloudGPT Ollama Family Catalog

This document tracks the high-level family taxonomy used by the NoCloudGPT deployment and SEO platform.

## Primary Goals

- Generate family-centric landing pages.
- Route visitors toward deployment offers.
- Simplify AI selection for Linux and private-cloud customers.
- Support Ollama-based deployments.
- Provide future expansion paths for automated quoting.

1. Customer lands on family page.
2. Customer selects intended use.
3. Customer receives hardware guidance.
4. Customer is routed to deployment offer.
5. Customer receives NoCloudGPT installation path.
