Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about how our private, self-hosted AI works — and why “no cloud” matters.

Private
Self-Hosted

1) Will it be “dumb”?

Not at all. We run strong open models locally (e.g., from Meta or Google-licensed open weights). Then we connect the model to your documents using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), so answers are grounded in your content — without sending data to external APIs.

2) Do you “train the model” on our data?

Most deployments use RAG: we privately index your files and fetch relevant snippets at answer time. This keeps results fresh and private. Full fine-tuning is possible for narrow or repetitive tasks, but usually unnecessary.

3) Where does it run?

On your own machine (office or home) or on a VM you control. The web UI is open-source and served from your box. No shared SaaS runtime.

4) Does any data leave our network?

By default, no. Models, indexes, and chat history remain on your hardware. Optional updates and backups can be configured to your approved destinations with encryption.

5) What documents can it use?

PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, Markdown, many HTML pages — plus sync from Google Drive, OneDrive/SharePoint, SMB/NAS, or S3 you control.

6) How private and secure is this?

Local compute, no external inference calls, optional SSO/MFA, role-based access per collection, LAN/VPN-only exposure, and encrypted backups. We’ll harden it with you.

7) What hardware do we need?

Great baseline: modern i7/Ryzen 7, 32–64GB RAM, fast NVMe. A 12–16GB NVIDIA GPU (e.g., 3060/4060) speeds things up. CPU-only is fine with smaller models.

8) Will it remember chats and cite sources?

Yes. Multi-user chat history lives locally, and answers can include citations back to your documents for easy verification.

9) RAG vs. training — what’s the real difference?

RAG = index + fetch snippets at answer time (fast, flexible, up-to-date). Training = change model weights (slower, costlier, static until retrained). We default to RAG.

10) Can it run fully offline?

Yes. After initial setup (models downloaded, docs indexed), it runs without internet. Reconnect when you want updates or to push encrypted backups.

11) How do updates and backups work?

Updates are a simple Docker refresh. Backups cover the vector index, chat DB, and config — to your NAS or S3-compatible storage with encryption and retention.

12) Is this legal/medical advice?

No. The system helps you search and summarize your own materials. You’re responsible for professional review and compliance.
