License rule

Glass Licenses are interchangeable. Deployment Keys are not.

A Glass License is the portable capacity customers buy. A Deployment Key is the revocable credential for one active installation or approved deployment.

  • No separate Jet License for launch.
  • Jet Agents are curated cloud-model lanes inside the package.
  • Glass Agents are OpenClaw-powered action workers.

Is this mainly about privacy?

Privacy matters, but the bigger sales value is choice: different models, different agent workflows, and a private computing path that avoids locking the business into one monolithic AI vendor experience.

Is a Jet Agent a separate license?

No. For launch, Glass Licenses are the sellable capacity unit. Jet Agents are curated cloud-model lanes available through the customer’s terminal.glass package and deployment.

Does terminal.glass need access to the customer server?

Not by default. The customer deployment activates outward to the terminal.glass license system. Customers chat on their own instance.

What happens when Alchemist Conductor launches?

Sunrise Group Plan customers should receive a major founding discount or free upgrade path, final offer TBD. Alchemist will coordinate Glass Agents and Jet Agents into approved business workflows.
