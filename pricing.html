License rule

Glass Licenses are portable. Deployment Keys are not.

A Glass License is the capacity customers buy from terminal.glass. A Deployment Key is the revocable credential for one active supported deployment.

  • One Glass License supports one active deployment at a time.
  • Licenses can be reassigned when needs change.
  • NoCloudGPT runs on customer-owned or customer-controlled infrastructure.

Choose your deployment path

NoCloudGPT supports several practical architectures. Pick the path that matches your privacy, connectivity, and operations requirements.

Air-gapped / on-premise

Maximum privacy. Run Ollama and your chat interface on hardware you own, with no required outbound cloud dependency.

  • Full data sovereignty
  • Ideal for compliance-sensitive environments
  • Works offline once models are present

AWS Lightsail / customer cloud

Customer-owned cloud instances when local hardware is not ready or a remote pilot is faster to launch.

  • Customer retains cloud account control
  • Good first rollout for many teams
  • Pairs with YourCloudGPT-style paths on terminal.glass

Private cloud / GPU cluster

Multi-node Linux, Proxmox, or dedicated GPU servers for larger teams and heavier model workloads.

  • Shared internal AI services
  • Better concurrency for reasoning and coding models
  • Scales with your infrastructure team

What to plan before you deploy

Hardware and storage

Model size, quantization, and concurrent users determine RAM, VRAM, and disk needs. Browse the model catalog for family-specific guidance.

Local versus cloud

Local deployments keep inference on your systems. Cloud deployments can still be customer-owned while using terminal.glass installers and activation.

Internet access

Air-gapped NoCloudGPT can run without outbound internet after models are downloaded. Activation and license checks follow your terminal.glass deployment path.

Data ownership

Prompts, documents, and model outputs remain on infrastructure you control. NoCloudGPT is an educational and technical brand; terminal.glass handles licensing and sales.
