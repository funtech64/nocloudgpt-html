License rule

Glass Licenses are portable. Deployment Keys are not.

A Glass License is the capacity customers buy from terminal.glass. A Deployment Key is the revocable credential for one active supported COLORS.chat deployment.

  • One Glass License supports one active deployment at a time.
  • Licenses can be reassigned when needs change.
  • COLORS.chat runs on customer-owned or customer-controlled infrastructure.

What to plan before you deploy

Hardware and storage

Model size, quantization, and concurrent users determine RAM, VRAM, and disk needs. Browse the model catalog for family-specific guidance.

Local versus cloud

NoCloudGPT keeps inference on your systems. YourCloudGPT can still be customer-owned while using terminal.glass installers and activation.

Internet access

Air-gapped COLORS.chat can run without outbound internet after models are downloaded. Activation follows your terminal.glass deployment path.

Data ownership

Prompts, documents, and model outputs remain on infrastructure you control. NoCloudGPT is the educational brand; terminal.glass handles licensing and sales.
