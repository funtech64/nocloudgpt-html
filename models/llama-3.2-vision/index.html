Process Images Alongside Text — Privately, On Your Own Server

Who developed it

Meta AI Research

[Meta AI Research — developer badge]

Llama 3.2 Vision was released by Meta AI in November 2024 alongside the broader Llama 3.2 release that included lightweight 1B and 3B text models. The vision variants extend Llama 3.1's architecture with a vision encoder trained on image-text data.

When to Choose a Different Model

You want the latest vision model

Llama 4 Scout includes native vision with a 10M token context. For new deployments, compare Llama 3.2 Vision against Llama 4. See Llama 4 →

Your primary need is text, not vision

Vision models require more hardware than text-only alternatives. If vision is occasional, a lighter text model with a separate vision endpoint may be more efficient. See Llama 3.3 →
