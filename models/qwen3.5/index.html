Qwen 3

Alibaba's Latest Generation — Dense and MoE Models From Pilot Scale to Production

Qwen 3 is Alibaba Cloud's newer generation of open-weight models, bringing both dense and Mixture-of-Experts variants at multiple sizes. The MoE variants deliver competitive quality at efficient inference cost. Strong multilingual performance, wide size range, and explicit reasoning support continue the Qwen tradition.

Who developed it

Alibaba Cloud — Qwen Research Team

Qwen 3 is developed by Alibaba Cloud's Qwen research team, following the Qwen 2.5 generation. The Qwen series has become one of the most comprehensive open-weight families, covering general language, coding, vision, and embedding models across a wide parameter range.

NoCloudGPT will confirm current model availability, license terms, tags, and hardware fit before deployment begins.Earlier Qwen generations used custom community licenses with specific commercial conditions.

Why teams choose it

Fast, Efficient Pre-Built Private AI

Widest size range of any open family

From sub-1B pilot models to large production deployments — all in the same family with consistent tooling, prompt formats, and upgrade paths.

MoE variants for capability at efficiency

Qwen 3 MoE variants activate only a subset of parameters per inference, delivering stronger output at lower active compute cost than equivalent dense models.

Native reasoning mode

Qwen 3 introduces a thinking mode that produces chain-of-thought reasoning similar to DeepSeek R1, making it versatile for both analytical and general-purpose workflows.

30+ language multilingual strength

Strong multilingual performance inherited and improved from Qwen 2.5, covering Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and many others.
