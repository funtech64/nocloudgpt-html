Aya: Cohere's Massively Multilingual Instruction Models

Multilingual 23+ Languages Instruction Open Weights

Aya is Cohere's open-weight model series focused on multilingual instruction following across 23 to 101 languages. Most open-weight models are trained predominantly on English data and handle other languages as an afterthought. Aya is designed from the ground up for multilingual use, drawing on the Aya Dataset — a community-built multilingual instruction dataset covering dozens of languages that lack strong representation in standard training corpora. Aya Expanse, the current flagship, delivers particularly strong multilingual performance at 8B and 32B scales.

What Aya Is Good At

  • Instruction following in 23+ languages — covers major world languages including Arabic, Turkish, Hindi, Swahili, Vietnamese, and many more
  • Cross-lingual Q&A and summarization — ask questions in one language, get answers grounded in multilingual context
  • Low-resource language support — languages underrepresented in most models get meaningful coverage here
  • Translation assistance — useful for translation workflows, though dedicated translation models may still outperform it on narrow pairs
  • Multilingual customer service and support — one model can serve users across many language markets
  • International document processing — summarize and extract information from non-English documents reliably

Who Should Care

Business and Professional Use

Aya is the right choice for organizations operating internationally where serving users in their native language matters. A single Aya deployment can handle customer queries in Arabic on Monday, Turkish on Tuesday, and Hindi on Wednesday — without maintaining separate models or paying per-query fees for translation APIs. Useful for: international e-commerce support, multilingual content moderation, global HR tools, and NGOs operating in diverse language markets.

Organizations working in African or South Asian language contexts will find Aya covers territory that most other open-weight models simply don't support well.

Personal and Homelab Use

Bilingual or multilingual homelab users who want a private assistant that actually performs well in their non-English language should evaluate Aya alongside Yi (Chinese), InternLM (Chinese), and DeepSeek before choosing.

NoCloudGPT Deployment Guidance

Small Cloud Server (Lightsail)

Aya Expanse 8B on a g4dn.xlarge (16GB VRAM) is a practical pilot for a multilingual team assistant. It handles the full 101-language range at a size that fits affordable cloud hardware: 

# Via Ollama (check for community GGUF in registry)
ollama run aya-expanse:8b

# Or directly with llama.cpp
./llama-server -m aya-expanse-8b-q4_k_m.gguf \
  --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8080 -ngl 40

Local Linux Machine

Aya Expanse 8B in Q4_K_M GGUF runs on a 12GB GPU with comfortable headroom. For CPU-only machines, 16–24GB RAM will handle it at 2–5 tokens/sec — usable for low-volume tasks.

On-Premise / Office Server

Aya Expanse 32B on a single A100 40GB or two A10G GPUs is a strong production multilingual deployment. Serve with vLLM: 

vllm serve CohereForAI/aya-expanse-32b \
  --tensor-parallel-size 2

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Aya better than using a translation API plus an English model?

For many real-world use cases, yes. Translation adds latency, cost, and a potential accuracy loss on nuanced content. Aya handles the full pipeline in one step and maintains better cultural and contextual understanding in non-English inputs.

Does Aya perform as well in all 101 languages?

No — coverage varies. Major world languages (Spanish, French, Arabic, Chinese, Hindi) are strongest. More obscure languages have fewer training examples and will produce less reliable output. Test your target language before production deployment.

What's Aya's license for commercial use?

Aya models use a research license (CC-BY-NC for some variants). Commercial deployment requires checking the specific model's Hugging Face license card — Cohere's terms have evolved across model versions.
