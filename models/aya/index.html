What this model family is

An Open-Weight Model Built for Genuinely Multilingual AI Work

Aya is a family of open-weight multilingual language models developed by Cohere. The Aya project started as a research initiative involving thousands of researchers worldwide building multilingual instruction data for underrepresented languages — languages that other AI models treat as afterthoughts. The resulting models cover 100+ languages with training data quality that reflects genuine multilingual depth, not just English translated into other languages.

For organizations with operations, customers, or content across multiple languages — particularly in regions that major AI providers underserve — Aya provides better output quality in those languages than general-purpose English-primary models.

Who developed it

Cohere — Aya Initiative

[Cohere / Aya — developer badge artwork]

Aya was developed by Cohere For AI, Cohere's nonprofit research organization, in collaboration with a large open research community. The Aya initiative aimed to address the significant gap in multilingual AI quality for underrepresented languages by building high-quality instruction datasets through community contribution and then training models on that data.

Who this model family is for

Four Perspectives on Deploying Aya

👤 Personal use

A private assistant that actually works in your language

If your daily life or study happens in a language English-first models handle poorly — Swahili, Hindi, Amharic, Vietnamese, and dozens more — Aya is one of the few open models trained to treat those languages as first-class. The 8B variant runs on a Standard AI instance, giving you a genuinely multilingual assistant on a single private server. Note that Aya's instruct models are non-commercial, which is fine for personal use.

🏢 Business & team use

Global teams serving genuinely multilingual markets

For organisations with customers or staff across many languages, Aya consolidates translation and AI assistance into one private deployment and outperforms English-first models on under-served languages. The critical caveat: the Aya 23 and Aya Expanse instruct models are CC-BY-NC (non-commercial). Commercial deployments need the Apache-2.0 Aya 101 or a different model — NoCloudGPT will confirm which fits.

⚙️ Developer & technical use

A multilingual backend where the license permits it

Aya is a useful engine for multilingual support bots, translation-assist tooling, and content workflows that span dozens of languages from a single endpoint. Because most current Aya instruct weights are non-commercial, developers building revenue-generating products should either use the Apache-2.0 Aya 101 or treat Aya as an evaluation/research baseline and ship on a permissively licensed multilingual model.

🔬 Data science & research

A flagship for multilingual and low-resource NLP research

Aya is, at its core, a research project: it was built through a transparent global effort to create high-quality instruction data for under-represented languages. For NLP researchers, NGOs, and academics studying multilingual AI, low-resource languages, or data provenance, Aya is both a strong tool and a well-documented dataset — and its non-commercial license fits research use cleanly.

Licensing information

License Summary

Aya licensing is the decisive factor for commercial use, and it varies by model. The headline instruct models — Aya 23 and Aya Expanse — are released under CC-BY-NC 4.0, which prohibits commercial use. The older Aya 101 (an mT5-based multilingual model) is Apache 2.0 and is the commercially usable option in the family. Confirm the exact variant before any revenue-generating deployment.
