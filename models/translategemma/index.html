⚖️ Law Firms and Legal Departments

International arbitration, cross-border M&A, and multi-jurisdictional litigation all require translating documents that are subject to legal privilege. Running those documents through a third-party translation API creates an uncontrolled disclosure risk. TranslateGemma on a private server keeps privileged communications in-house, with no third-party data processor in the chain.

Translating term sheets, credit agreements, regulatory correspondence, and customer KYC documents involves non-public financial information. Most retail translation APIs are not designed to meet SEC, FINRA, or FCA data handling requirements. A self-hosted TranslateGemma instance runs under your existing data governance framework — the same controls you apply to any other internal system.

Organizations translating testimony, leaked documents, or source communications face real risks from commercial API providers who may be subject to legal orders in multiple jurisdictions. Deploy in Under an Hour

  1. 1. Create an AWS Lightsail 4 GB instance running Ubuntu 22.04 LTS. For higher throughput, choose 8 GB.
  2. 2. Install Ollama: curl -fsSL https://ollama.com/install.sh | sh
  3. 3. Pull the model: ollama pull translategemma
  4. 4. Lock down your Lightsail firewall to your office IP or VPN CIDR — translation requests stay on your private network.
  5. 5. Call the API from your internal tool or document pipeline: POST http://localhost:11434/api/generate with your source text and target language in the prompt.
