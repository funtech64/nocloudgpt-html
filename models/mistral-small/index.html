Mistral Small

The Enterprise Balance Point — Stronger Reasoning on Professional GPU Hardware

Mistral Small (22B parameters) is the tier between Mistral Nemo's document-processing efficiency and Mistral Large's frontier quality. It delivers substantially stronger reasoning on complex analysis tasks while remaining practical on professional workstation GPUs — an RTX 4080, 4090, or A4000.
