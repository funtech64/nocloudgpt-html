SeaLLM

Southeast Asian Language AI — Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, and More, Privately Deployed

SeaLLM is a language model developed specifically for Southeast Asian languages — Thai, Vietnamese, Bahasa Indonesia, Malay, Filipino (Tagalog), Burmese, Khmer, Lao, and others that are systematically underserved by globally trained models. For organizations operating in Southeast Asia, SeaLLM delivers meaningfully better language quality than English-primary alternatives.

Who developed it

DAMO Academy / Alibaba

[DAMO Academy / Alibaba — developer badge]

SeaLLM was developed by DAMO Academy, the AI research division of Alibaba Group. The project aimed specifically at underserved Southeast Asian languages that receive minimal training data in globally distributed AI models, resulting in significantly better performance in those languages compared to English-first alternatives.

Why teams choose it

Fast, Efficient Pre-Built Private AI

Languages that global models underserve

Thai, Vietnamese, Bahasa Indonesia, Malay, Filipino, Burmese, Khmer, and Lao receive far less training data in globally deployed models. SeaLLM's targeted training produces meaningfully better output in these languages.

Private handling of Southeast Asian content
