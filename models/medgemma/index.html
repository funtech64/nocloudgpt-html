What MedGemma Does

Medical Image Understanding

Analyze chest X-rays, CT slices, dermatology photos, and pathology slides. MedGemma's vision encoder is pre-trained on diverse biomedical imaging corpora — it recognizes anatomical structures and common findings out of the box, without extra fine-tuning for basic use cases.

Clinical Text Comprehension

Summarize discharge summaries, extract ICD-relevant findings from clinical notes, answer questions about lab reports, and draft structured clinical documentation. MedGemma understands medical abbreviations, drug names, and diagnostic terminology that general-purpose models struggle with.

Multimodal Clinical Reasoning

Combine image and text in a single prompt. Ask "Given this chest X-ray and the patient's reported symptoms, what findings are present and what differentials should be considered?" — and receive a structured, medically grounded response entirely within your private environment.

Model Variants and Recommended Hardware

Variant Parameters Min RAM Recommended Deployment Best For
MedGemma 4B (text) 4B 4 GB Lightsail 4 GB Clinical text summarization, note Q&A
MedGemma 4B (multimodal) 4B 6 GB Lightsail 8 GB Image + text, radiology triage
MedGemma 27B (multimodal) 27B 20 GB+ GPU instance / on-premise High-accuracy research, complex multimodal reasoning

For most clinical workflow pilots, MedGemma 4B multimodal on Lightsail 8 GB is the right starting point. It runs at Q4 quantization (~5–6 GB RAM) without GPU acceleration and handles real-time image + text queries at acceptable clinical-support latency. The 27B variant requires a GPU-equipped instance (A10G or better) for production throughput.

Privacy and Compliance Posture

No PHI to Third Parties

Because MedGemma runs entirely on your infrastructure via Ollama, patient images and clinical notes never leave your environment. There's no API call to Google, no model-provider data retention, and no ambiguity about where your data goes.

HIPAA-Aligned Architecture

AWS Lightsail instances can be deployed in HIPAA-eligible regions under your existing AWS Business Associate Agreement. Combine with encrypted EBS volumes and VPC isolation for a defensible HIPAA technical safeguard posture — without standing up a complex managed AI service.

Auditable Inference Logs

Every query and response can be logged locally to structured JSON. Your compliance team gets a complete audit trail of AI-assisted clinical decisions — query, image hash, response, timestamp — without exporting sensitive data to a vendor's logging infrastructure.

Fine-Tuning on Your Data

MedGemma's open weights mean you can fine-tune the model on your institution's labeled imaging dataset using LoRA adapters — then deploy the adapted model privately. Your institution's clinical knowledge stays yours, not incorporated into a shared vendor model.

⚠️ Clinical Decision Support — Not a Medical Device

MedGemma is a research and clinical decision support tool. It is not FDA-cleared or CE-marked as a medical device and should not be used as the sole basis for diagnostic or treatment decisions. All AI-generated clinical outputs must be reviewed by qualified healthcare professionals. Confirm your specific regulatory obligations with your compliance and legal teams before deployment.

Getting Started in Under an Hour

  1. 1. Provision an AWS Lightsail 8 GB instance (Ubuntu 22.04 LTS) in your preferred HIPAA-eligible region.

  3. 3. Pull the multimodal variant: ollama pull medgemma
  4. 4. Restrict network access to your VPN or internal IP range via Lightsail firewall rules.
  5. 5. Connect your clinical application or internal tool to http://localhost:11434 via Ollama's REST API.
