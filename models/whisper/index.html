What Is Whisper?

Whisper is an automatic speech recognition (ASR) model released by OpenAI in September 2022. Unlike most of the models on this site, Whisper is not a language model — it's a transformer-based encoder-decoder trained specifically to convert spoken audio into text. It was trained on 680,000 hours of multilingual, multitask supervised data scraped from the web — one of the largest and most diverse ASR training datasets assembled.

The result is a model that handles real-world audio conditions exceptionally well: background noise, accents, multiple languages, technical vocabulary, and inconsistent recording quality. Where purpose-built ASR systems tuned for clean studio audio struggle with real meeting recordings or field audio, Whisper is robust because it was trained on the kind of messy audio that actually exists in the world.

Crucially, Whisper's weights are fully open. You can download and run it locally — which means your audio never needs to touch OpenAI's servers, your meeting recordings stay on your hardware, and transcription happens at the rate your local GPU or CPU can process it, with no per-minute API costs.

The privacy case for local Whisper: Cloud transcription services — including OpenAI's own Whisper API — receive your audio. For legal consultations, medical discussions, financial calls, internal strategy meetings, and personal recordings, sending audio to a third-party server is a significant data exposure. Local Whisper eliminates this entirely. The same model, the same accuracy, zero external data transmission.

Whisper Model Variants

Whisper is available in five sizes. Larger models are more accurate — especially on difficult audio — but require more compute and take longer to transcribe. The right choice depends on your accuracy requirements and available hardware.

Whisper Tiny

39M Parameters

Fastest transcription, lowest accuracy. Useful for rapid pre-screening of audio content or extremely resource-constrained environments. Not recommended for anything requiring high accuracy.

Whisper Base

74M Parameters

A step up from Tiny. Suitable for clean audio with clear speech. Low hardware requirements, reasonable speed. Good starting point for testing or low-stakes transcription workflows.

Whisper Small

244M Parameters

The practical sweet spot for many use cases. Meaningfully better than Base on challenging audio while still being fast on modest hardware. Good choice for general meeting transcription on CPU.

Whisper Medium

769M Parameters

Strong accuracy across languages and audio conditions. Recommended for professional transcription workflows, multilingual content, and situations where accuracy matters. Runs well on mid-range GPUs.

Whisper Large v3

1.5B Parameters

The flagship model. Best accuracy available in the Whisper family, with improved multilingual performance over v2. For production transcription pipelines where quality is the priority. Requires a capable GPU for real-time performance.

Whisper Large v3 Turbo

809M Parameters

A distilled version of Large v3 — faster and more efficient while retaining most of the accuracy. The balanced fit of quality and speed for most production deployments. Often the right choice over full Large v3.

What Whisper Does Well

Real-World Audio Robustness

Whisper was trained on internet-scale audio — not curated studio recordings. This means it handles the audio quality that actually exists: conference room reverb, laptop microphones, phone calls, outdoor recordings with wind noise, and accented speech. For meeting transcription and field recording use cases, this robustness is decisive.

Multilingual Transcription

Whisper supports transcription in 99 languages and can translate non-English audio directly to English text in a single pass. This makes it uniquely useful for international organizations, multilingual research teams, and applications dealing with audio content in multiple languages — all processed locally with no third-party language data exposure.

Timestamps and Segmentation

Whisper outputs word-level and segment-level timestamps alongside transcription text. This enables downstream applications to link transcribed text back to specific moments in source audio — critical for search indexing, compliance review, meeting summaries linked to audio position, and subtitle generation.

Batch Processing

Unlike cloud APIs with rate limits and per-minute costs, local Whisper runs as fast as your hardware allows on as many files as you queue. For organizations with large archives of audio to transcribe — legal depositions, recorded sales calls, training materials, research interviews — batch processing locally eliminates both the cost and the data exposure of cloud services.

Who This Model Family is For

Personal Use

Whisper is an excellent choice if you record voice memos, lectures, podcasts, interviews, or meetings and want accurate transcription without uploading your audio to any cloud service. It handles real-world audio well — including accents, background noise, and casual speech — and produces clean, timestamped text. It is a specialized transcription tool, not a chatbot, so it will not summarize your recordings or answer questions about them on its own.

Business / Team Use

Whisper is particularly valuable for organizations that handle sensitive audio — such as client calls, internal meetings, medical or legal dictation, and HR interviews — where sending recordings to a third-party API creates compliance or confidentiality risks. Running it locally through NoCloudGPT keeps all audio inside your infrastructure. It delivers reliable, timestamped transcripts that can be fed into downstream workflows, though you will need a separate model or tool if you also require summaries or action items.

Developer & Technical Use

Developers can use Whisper as a self-hosted speech-to-text engine for transcription features, voice interfaces, subtitling pipelines, and call analytics tools. It provides clean, predictable text output that is easy to integrate into larger systems, with strong multilingual support across nearly 100 languages. Because it is purpose-built for automatic speech recognition (ASR), integration is straightforward: send audio in, receive transcribed (and optionally translated) text out.

Data Science & Research Use

Researchers working with audio corpora — including qualitative interviews, field recordings, linguistic datasets, or oral histories — will find Whisper highly effective for producing accurate transcripts across many languages without per-minute API costs or data-sharing concerns. Its robustness to noisy, real-world recordings makes it practical for messy source material. It is a transcription and translation tool, so it is best paired with other models for analysis, coding, or NLP tasks downstream.

Deploying Whisper via Open WebUI and Ollama

Whisper Integration in Open WebUI

Open WebUI includes native Whisper integration for voice input — enabling speech-to-text directly in the browser chat interface. When you speak to Open WebUI, your audio is transcribed locally by Whisper before being sent to your local LLM. No audio touches external servers at any point in the pipeline.

# Install faster-whisper for standalone transcription
pip install faster-whisper

# Transcribe a file with Large v3 Turbo
python3 -c "
from faster_whisper import WhisperModel
model = WhisperModel('large-v3-turbo', device='cuda')
segments, info = model.transcribe('meeting.mp3')
for segment in segments:
  print(f'[{segment.start:.1f}s] {segment.text}')
"

# Open WebUI with Whisper STT enabled
docker run -d -p 3000:8080 \
  -e OLLAMA_BASE_URL=http://localhost:11434 \
  -e WHISPER_MODEL=base \
  ghcr.io/open-webui/open-webui:main
