What Are OpenHermes & Nous Hermes?

The Hermes family of models originates from Nous Research, an open-source AI lab focused on building high-quality fine-tunes of foundational models. Rather than training from scratch, Nous Research applies rigorous dataset curation and supervised fine-tuning (SFT) techniques to base models — primarily Mistral and Llama variants — to produce assistants that dramatically outperform their base counterparts on instruction-following benchmarks.

OpenHermes 2.5 (fine-tuned on Mistral 7B) became a community landmark by demonstrating that a 7B-parameter model, when trained on a high-quality synthetic dataset of roughly one million samples, could match or exceed much larger models on coding, reasoning, and multi-turn conversation tasks. Its successor variants have continued this trajectory.

Nous Hermes 2 extended this approach to larger base models including Llama 2 13B and Mixtral 8x7B, offering enterprise-grade instruction quality at open-weight sizes. These models are especially well-suited for private deployments where you need reliable, nuanced assistant behavior without depending on external APIs or commercial model agreements.

Hermes vs. Base Models

A common question: why deploy a Hermes fine-tune rather than the underlying base model (Mistral 7B or Llama 3.1 8B)?

Base models are powerful but require careful prompting and often produce unpredictable behavior for instruction-following tasks. Hermes fine-tuning addresses this directly — the model has been trained on hundreds of thousands of high-quality instruction examples, dramatically improving response quality, formatting consistency, and task completion rates without any additional prompt engineering on your part.

For production assistant deployments — where reliability and consistency matter more than raw benchmark scores — a well-trained fine-tune like OpenHermes will outperform a larger, less-tuned base model in the majority of real-world use cases. This is the philosophy behind the Nous Research approach, and it has been validated by the community across thousands of real deployments.
