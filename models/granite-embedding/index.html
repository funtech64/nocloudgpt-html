IBM's Enterprise Data Governance, Applied to Embeddings

IBM's Granite model family was built with a specific organizational reality in mind: enterprises in regulated industries cannot simply deploy the most performant available model without understanding and documenting what data it was trained on. Legal risk from training data provenance — particularly around copyright, data consent, and intellectual property — is a genuine concern that blocks AI adoption at many large organizations.

IBM addressed this directly. The Granite models, including Granite Embedding, are trained on data that IBM has curated with explicit attention to business-safe provenance. IBM publishes what categories of data were used, what was excluded, and what governance processes were applied. The Apache 2.0 license covers commercial deployment without carve-outs or usage restrictions that other "open" models sometimes include in their license fine print.

For organizations where the AI procurement team, legal counsel, and compliance officers all need to sign off on an embedding model, Granite Embedding offers a documented answer to each of their questions. The performance is competitive. The governance story is unmatched in the open-weight embedding space.
