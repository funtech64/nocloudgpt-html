Sailor2

Inclusive Multilingual AI for Southeast Asia — Privately Deployed

Sailor2 is a community-driven family of multilingual language models built for South-East Asian languages — Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Tagalog, Burmese, Khmer, Lao, and more. Built on Qwen 2.5 and continuously pre-trained for regional fluency, Sailor2 delivers natural, culturally aware output that global models often miss.

Who developed it

Sea AI Lab & Sailor2 Community

Sailor2 is a collaborative initiative led by Sea AI Lab with partners across the Southeast Asian AI community. The models are continuously pre-trained on hundreds of billions of tokens to improve coverage of underserved regional languages while retaining strong English and Chinese capability.

Who this model family is for

Who This Model Family is For

👤 Personal use

Sailor2 is a strong choice if you regularly communicate in Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, or other Southeast Asian languages and want a private AI that understands regional phrasing, tone, and cultural context. It performs well for drafting messages, summarizing local content, or having natural conversations in your native language. If your primary language is English, you will generally get better overall results from a strong generalist model such as Qwen 2.5 or Llama 3.3.

🏢 Business / team use

Teams operating across Southeast Asian markets will find Sailor2 useful for customer support, localized content creation, and internal communications in multiple regional languages. It captures nuance and tone that make messages feel natural rather than translated. Running it through NoCloudGPT keeps sensitive customer data and internal documents entirely within your infrastructure. For primarily English-language or analytically heavy workflows, a generalist model is usually the better default.

⚙️ Developer & technical use

Developers building products for Southeast Asian users — such as multilingual chatbots, localization tools, or regional content platforms — can use Sailor2 as a capable regional language engine. It integrates cleanly through Ollama and performs well when authentic Southeast Asian language output is a priority. For English-heavy logic, code generation, or tool-calling tasks, newer generalist models will typically deliver stronger results.

🔬 Data science & research

Researchers working on Southeast Asian NLP, regional discourse, or low-resource language modeling will find Sailor2 a practical, purpose-built model. Its focused training on the region's languages and scripts makes it useful for benchmarking, dataset work, and studying how cultural context influences model behavior. It is best treated as a specialist tool within its regional scope rather than a general-purpose reasoning model.
