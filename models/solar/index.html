Solar: Upstage's Depth-Upscaled Instruction Models

Instruction Document AI 10.7B Open Weights

Solar is an open-weight model family from Upstage, a Korean AI company known for its document understanding products. The flagship Solar 10.7B uses a technique called depth upscaling — essentially merging two Mistral 7B models and continuing training — to produce a 10.7B model that punches well above its weight class. When released, it topped the HuggingFace Open LLM Leaderboard for its size tier. Solar Pro is a larger, higher-capability variant for enterprise deployments.

What Solar Is Good At

  • Instruction following and chat — Solar Instruct is notably well-aligned and follows complex instructions reliably
  • Document understanding — Upstage's document AI roots show; Solar handles structured and semi-structured document Q&A well
  • Reasoning — strong benchmark performance on reasoning tasks for a sub-13B model
  • RAG integration — clean instruction following makes it easy to build retrieval pipelines around
  • Korean and English — bilingual capability with solid Korean output quality

Who This Model Family Is For

👤 Personal use

Solar is a strong choice if you want a capable, locally-run assistant that doesn't demand high-end server hardware. Upstage's depth-upscaling approach delivers reasoning quality that punches above its parameter count, making it practical on a well-specced home machine or workstation. It handles writing, summarization, Q&A, and light coding effectively without the sluggishness often associated with larger models. If you've found smaller models too limited and larger models too slow or resource-heavy, Solar often strikes a useful balance.

🏢 Business & team use

Solar is well-suited for teams that need a reliable, self-hosted assistant for internal tasks such as drafting documents, summarizing reports, answering policy questions, or processing structured data — without sending sensitive information to external APIs. Its inference efficiency means you can serve multiple users from a single modest server. Upstage trained Solar with a focus on instruction-following accuracy, which translates to more consistent and predictable outputs in production workflows. It serves as a practical foundation for both internal tools and customer-facing applications where reliability matters more than occasional peak performance.

⚙️ Developer & technical use

Developers building RAG pipelines, internal tooling, or document-processing applications will find Solar responsive and predictable. It follows structured prompts reliably, which helps reduce prompt-engineering overhead during development. While it is not the top choice for cutting-edge code synthesis, it performs solidly on code explanation, boilerplate generation, and debugging assistance. Its efficiency also makes local testing and iteration faster, shortening the feedback loop during development.

🔬 Data science & research

Researchers and data scientists working with text-heavy datasets will appreciate Solar's strong summarization and reasoning capabilities relative to its computational footprint. It is a credible option for NLP preprocessing, annotation assistance, and exploratory analysis of unstructured documents — all running entirely on your own infrastructure. Because it is fully self-hosted via NoCloudGPT, there are no API rate limits or data-sharing concerns. If your work requires state-of-the-art benchmark performance or very long context windows, you may want to evaluate larger models alongside Solar.

NoCloudGPT Deployment Guidance

Small Cloud Server

Solar 10.7B Instruct runs comfortably on a g4dn.xlarge (16GB VRAM). Use Ollama: 

ollama run solar:10.7b-instruct-v1-q4_K_M

Local Linux Machine

Q4_K_M GGUF fits easily on a 12GB GPU. For CPU-only machines, the GGUF runs acceptably on 16GB RAM systems via llama.cpp.

On-Premise Server

Solar Pro (22B) runs well on a single A10G (24GB) or two RTX 4090s. Serve with vLLM for multi-user access.
