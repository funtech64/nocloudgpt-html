Solar: Upstage's Depth-Upscaled Instruction Models

Instruction Document AI 10.7B Open Weights

Solar is an open-weight model family from Upstage, a Korean AI company known for its document understanding products. The flagship Solar 10.7B uses a technique called depth upscaling — essentially merging two Mistral 7B models and continuing training — to produce a 10.7B model that punches well above its weight class. When released, it topped the HuggingFace Open LLM Leaderboard for its size tier. Solar Pro is a larger, higher-capability variant for enterprise deployments.

What Solar Is Good At

  • Instruction following and chat — Solar Instruct is notably well-aligned and follows complex instructions reliably
  • Document understanding — Upstage's document AI roots show; Solar handles structured and semi-structured document Q&A well
  • Reasoning — strong benchmark performance on reasoning tasks for a sub-13B model
  • RAG integration — clean instruction following makes it easy to build retrieval pipelines around
  • Korean and English — bilingual capability with solid Korean output quality

Who Should Care

Business and Professional Use

Solar 10.7B Instruct fits on a single 16GB GPU and produces output quality that competes with models twice its size. For teams that want a private document assistant or internal chatbot without investing in high-end GPU hardware, it's an efficient choice. It's particularly worth evaluating for workflows that involve Korean language content alongside English.

Personal and Homelab Use

Solar 10.7B in 4-bit quantization runs on an RTX 3060 (12GB) or RTX 3070 (8GB with slight compromise). It's a satisfying homelab model — better than most 7B alternatives, smaller than a 13B.

NoCloudGPT Deployment Guidance

Small Cloud Server

Solar 10.7B Instruct runs comfortably on a g4dn.xlarge (16GB VRAM). Use Ollama: 

ollama run solar:10.7b-instruct-v1-q4_K_M

Local Linux Machine

Q4_K_M GGUF fits easily on a 12GB GPU. For CPU-only machines, the GGUF runs acceptably on 16GB RAM systems via llama.cpp.

On-Premise Server

Solar Pro (22B) runs well on a single A10G (24GB) or two RTX 4090s. Serve with vLLM for multi-user access.
