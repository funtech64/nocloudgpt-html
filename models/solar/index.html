Solar: Upstage's Depth-Upscaled Instruction Models

Instruction Document AI 10.7B Open Weights

Solar is an open-weight model family from Upstage, a Korean AI company known for its document understanding products. The flagship Solar 10.7B uses a technique called depth upscaling — essentially merging two Mistral 7B models and continuing training — to produce a 10.7B model that punches well above its weight class. When released, it topped the HuggingFace Open LLM Leaderboard for its size tier. Solar Pro is a larger, higher-capability variant for enterprise deployments.

What Solar Is Good At

  • Instruction following and chat — Solar Instruct is notably well-aligned and follows complex instructions reliably
  • Document understanding — Upstage's document AI roots show; Solar handles structured and semi-structured document Q&A well
  • Reasoning — strong benchmark performance on reasoning tasks for a sub-13B model
  • RAG integration — clean instruction following makes it easy to build retrieval pipelines around
  • Korean and English — bilingual capability with solid Korean output quality

Who This Model Family Is For

👤 Personal use

Solar 10.7B in 4-bit quantization runs on an RTX 3060 (12GB) — or an RTX 3070 8GB with slight compromise — making it a satisfying homelab model that is noticeably better than most 7B alternatives while staying smaller than a 13B. It is a good personal pick if you want a single capable assistant, and especially if your day includes Korean alongside English. For commercial side projects, use the Apache-2.0 base rather than the non-commercial Instruct weights.

🏢 Business & team use

Solar 10.7B Instruct fits on a single 16GB GPU and produces output quality that competes with models twice its size, which makes it efficient for a private document assistant or internal chatbot without high-end hardware. It is particularly worth evaluating for Korean-and-English workflows. Important: the Instruct weights are CC-BY-NC (non-commercial) — for commercial production, plan on Solar Pro or a commercially licensed alternative.

⚙️ Developer & technical use

Solar's clean instruction following makes it straightforward to build RAG pipelines and document-extraction tooling around, and Upstage's document-AI heritage shows in structured-document handling. Developers should pick the variant by license: the SOLAR-10.7B base is Apache 2.0 for embedding in products, while the Instruct fine-tune is non-commercial. Confirm the terms before shipping.

🔬 Data science & research

Solar is a clean demonstration of depth upscaling — merging two Mistral 7B models and continuing pretraining — which makes it an interesting reference for researchers studying model-merging and efficient scaling. Its strong reasoning-per-parameter and bilingual Korean-English behaviour also make it a useful baseline for document-AI and cross-lingual evaluation work.

Licensing Information

Solar's license varies by variant, and the distinction matters for commercial use. The SOLAR-10.7B base model is Apache 2.0 (commercial-friendly), but the popular SOLAR-10.7B-Instruct is released under CC-BY-NC 4.0, which prohibits commercial use. Solar Pro Preview is released under MIT. Pick the variant whose license matches your intended use.
