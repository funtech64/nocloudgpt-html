PaliGemma: Google's Efficient Vision Model for Private Deployment

PaliGemma is Google's open-weight vision-language model built for specialized tasks: image captioning, object detection, visual question answering, and scene understanding. It runs on just 4GB of RAM, making it one of the most cost-efficient vision models available for private infrastructure — no Google Cloud account required.

What Is PaliGemma

PaliGemma combines Google's SigLIP vision encoder with a Gemma 2B language model. The design is deliberately task-focused: rather than trying to be a general-purpose conversational assistant, PaliGemma excels at structured vision tasks — answering specific questions about images, generating accurate captions, detecting and localizing objects, and performing document understanding on visually rich inputs.

This focused architecture means it performs at a level competitive with much larger models on captioning and detection benchmarks, while requiring a fraction of the compute. The 3B parameter model at Q4 quantization fits comfortably within 4GB of RAM — deployable on a Lightsail 4GB instance at roughly $16–20/month.

For organizations that don't need open-ended visual conversation but do need high-accuracy image understanding in a private, auditable environment, PaliGemma is often the most economical choice in the vision model tier.

Core Capabilities

🏷️

Image Captioning

Generate accurate, natural-language descriptions of images at scale. PaliGemma's SigLIP encoder captures fine-grained visual details, and the Gemma language backbone produces fluent, well-structured captions. Use it to auto-generate alt text for accessibility compliance, metadata for media libraries, or descriptions for e-commerce product images without manual review queues.

🔲

Object Detection & Localization

PaliGemma can identify and locate objects within images, outputting bounding box coordinates alongside class labels. This makes it useful for visual inspection workflows, inventory systems, safety compliance checks (e.g., PPE detection in facility photos), and content tagging pipelines that need spatial awareness, not just category labels.

❓

Visual Question Answering

Ask PaliGemma specific, targeted questions about an image and receive concise, accurate answers. "What color is the roof?" "How many items are on the shelf?" "Is the circuit board visibly damaged?" This makes it highly practical for structured pipelines where discrete questions replace open-ended analysis — faster, cheaper, and easier to validate.

📑

Document & Scene Understanding

Process images of documents, signage, charts, and mixed text-visual content. PaliGemma handles scene text recognition alongside semantic understanding — it can read visible text in an image and reason about the surrounding context, making it useful for retail shelf analysis, document classification, and visual content moderation workflows.

🗂️

Automated Image Classification

Route images into categories, flag anomalies, or score content against defined criteria — all described in natural language prompts rather than trained classifiers. PaliGemma's zero-shot performance on classification tasks means you can deploy new categorization logic without retraining, just by updating your prompt definitions.

♿

Accessibility & Compliance Pipelines

Generate WCAG-compliant alt text for image-heavy web properties, internal documentation systems, or content management platforms. PaliGemma can be integrated into publishing workflows to auto-tag images before they reach production — reducing manual accessibility review effort while keeping image content within your network.

Who Should Deploy PaliGemma

▸

E-commerce and media teams managing large image libraries who need automated captioning, alt text generation, and product image tagging without sending catalog images to third-party APIs.

▸

Facilities and operations teams running visual inspection workflows — equipment photos, safety audits, inventory checks — who want a lightweight, private detection model that doesn't require GPU infrastructure.

Content and accessibility teams at organizations with WCAG compliance obligations who need to process historical image archives or maintain ongoing alt text generation for new content.

▸

Developers building structured vision pipelines who need a reliable, fast model for discrete VQA tasks — where the answer is a word, a number, or a bounding box — rather than open-ended visual conversation.

Budget-conscious teams who want production-grade vision capability without the cost of 8B+ model compute. PaliGemma on a 4GB Lightsail instance is among the lowest-cost private vision deployments available.

Deployment Configuration

PaliGemma is available via Ollama. The 3B model at Q4 quantization requires approximately 3–4GB of RAM, fitting cleanly on a Lightsail 4GB instance. For higher throughput or concurrent users, the 4GB instance should be treated as single-user or low-frequency use; upgrade to 8GB for team-level deployments.
