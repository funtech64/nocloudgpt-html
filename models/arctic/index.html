Enterprise Analytics AI Built for SQL, Data, and Business Intelligence — On Your Infrastructure

Purpose-built for enterprise analytics, SQL generation, and business intelligence tasks, it is released under Apache 2.0 and optimized specifically for the data analysis workloads that Snowflake's customers use most.

Who developed it

Snowflake

Snowflake Arctic was released by Snowflake in April 2024. Snowflake is a cloud data platform company whose customers primarily use SQL and business analytics workloads. Arctic was built with those use cases in mind — producing a model that excels at SQL generation, data analysis, and enterprise intelligence tasks over general-purpose conversation.
