Where COLORS.chat runs

Deployment paths for your private workspace.

COLORS.chat is the product. NoCloudGPT is the local/private path. YourCloudGPT is the customer-cloud path. Purchase Glass Licenses on terminal.glass/pricing/ or order on colors.chat.

NoCloudGPT

Mac / on-premise Linux

Deploy COLORS.chat on Ubuntu or Debian servers you own — office Linux, homelab, clinic, or air-gapped environments.

YourCloudGPT

AWS Lightsail / customer cloud

Low-cost pilots when local hardware is not ready. You retain the cloud account.

Scale-up

Private cloud

Multi-node Proxmox, QEMU, and GPU systems for teams that need shared internal COLORS.chat services.
COLORS.chat is what teams open after install — powered by colorsXUI. Glass Agents and Jet Agents attach through terminal.glass.

Typical deployment flow

  1. Evaluate model and hardware fit in the NoCloudGPT catalog.
  2. Order on colors.chat or purchase Glass Licenses on terminal.glass.
  3. Choose NoCloudGPT, YourCloudGPT, or private-cloud architecture.
  4. Run the supported installer or bootstrap path in your environment.
  5. Activate the deployment to your terminal.glass license dashboard.
