NoCloudGPT deployment paths

NoCloudGPT is the local and private deployment option within terminal.glass. Choose where your AI should live, plan hardware and model fit here, then purchase supported Glass Licenses on terminal.glass/pricing/.

On your hardware

On-Premise Linux

Deploy Ollama on Ubuntu or Debian servers you own — office Linux, homelab, clinic, or air-gapped environments.

Customer cloud

AWS Lightsail

Low-cost pilot deployments when local hardware is not ready. You retain the cloud account.

Scale-up

Private Cloud

Multi-node Proxmox, QEMU, and GPU systems for teams that need shared internal AI services.

Typical deployment flow

  1. Evaluate model and hardware fit in the NoCloudGPT catalog.
  2. Purchase Glass License packages on terminal.glass.
  3. Choose on-premise, Lightsail, or private cloud architecture.
  4. Run the supported installer or bootstrap path in your environment.
  5. Activate the deployment to your terminal.glass license dashboard.

Ready for packages and pricing?
