Gemma 2

Google's Most Deployment-Tested Generation — Proven, Efficient, and Runs on a 2 GB Instance

Gemma 2 is Google DeepMind's second-generation open-weight family, available at 2B and 9B sizes. Extensively tested since its mid-2024 release, Gemma 2 is the most stable and well-optimized Gemma generation for production deployment — running on Starter AI 2 GB instances at the small end and Standard AI 4 GB at the 9B size.

Who developed it

Google DeepMind

Gemma 2 was released by Google DeepMind in mid-2024 with 2B and 9B sizes, and a 27B variant. It introduced improved knowledge distillation training that produces a 2B model with unusually strong instruction following for its size. Since release, Gemma 2 has accumulated significant community testing and optimization in the Ollama ecosystem.

Who this model family is for

Four Perspectives on Deploying Gemma 2

👤 Personal use

The cheapest way to run a real private assistant

Gemma 2B runs on a 2 GB instance, so it is the most affordable route to a private AI you actually own — useful for anyone who wants a ChatGPT-style helper for drafting, summarising, and everyday questions without monthly fees or data leaving their server. Step up to 9B on a 4 GB instance when you want clearly better answers; the experience and tooling stay the same.

🏢 Business & team use

A proven, low-cost first deployment for small teams

Gemma 2 has been in the Ollama ecosystem since mid-2024, so it is well-tested and predictable — exactly what you want for a first production rollout. The 9B on a 4 GB instance covers business writing, summarisation, and internal Q&A at a price point that makes replacing SaaS subscriptions an easy case to justify to a budget owner.

⚙️ Developer & technical use

Reliable instruction following for structured output

Gemma 2 is notably good at following system prompts and holding format constraints, which makes it a dependable backend for templated workflows, classification, and JSON-style structured output where a model that wanders off-format causes real problems. The small footprint also makes it easy to run several instances cheaply for higher throughput.

🔬 Data science & research

A stable, well-documented baseline

For researchers and data teams who need a dependable small-model baseline rather than the newest release, Gemma 2's maturity is the point: extensive community testing, stable quantizations, and well-understood behaviour make it a solid control to benchmark against or a low-cost workhorse for batch classification and labelling tasks on modest hardware.

Licensing information

License Summary

All Gemma 2 sizes are released under the Gemma Terms of Use, Google's custom open-weight license. It permits commercial use and redistribution but is not an OSI-approved open-source license: it carries a Prohibited Use Policy and attribution requirements that apply to the model and to anything you build on it. The same terms apply across 2B, 9B, and 27B — review the policy before commercial deployment.
