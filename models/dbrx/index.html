DBRX

Databricks' Apache 2.0 Enterprise AI — 132B MoE Reasoning on Your Own Infrastructure

DBRX is Databricks' open-source 132B Mixture-of-Experts model. Released March 2024 under Apache 2.0, it delivers frontier-quality reasoning for enterprise analytics, data science, and coding workflows — with the training data transparency and IBM-adjacent enterprise accountability that regulated industries require.

Who developed it

Databricks

[Databricks — developer badge]

DBRX was released by Databricks in March 2024 as a 132B parameter MoE model (36B active per token). Databricks is a data and AI company known for Apache Spark, Delta Lake, and the MLflow platform. The company trained DBRX on carefully curated data with documented provenance — an enterprise-grade commitment that distinguishes it from models with opaque training data.

Why teams choose it

Fast, Efficient Pre-Built Private AI

Apache 2.0 at 132B

The largest commonly-deployed open model under Apache 2.0. Clean commercial licensing for enterprise integrations without Meta's custom license complexity.

MoE efficiency at enterprise scale

36B active parameters out of 132B total — near-frontier reasoning quality at lower inference compute cost than an equivalent dense model.

Data provenance documentation

Databricks documented DBRX's training data sources — a meaningful advantage for regulated industries that need to answer questions about what their AI was trained on.

Coding and data analysis strength

Databricks' background in data and ML tooling shows in DBRX's performance on code generation, SQL, and analytical reasoning tasks relevant to data engineering teams.
