Section 3

Safety & Guard Models

Inline classifiers that screen what goes into your AI pipelines and what comes out. Deploy these alongside your generative model — no external moderation API required. Your users' inputs and your model's outputs are evaluated entirely within your infrastructure.

Safety models are not replacements for human oversight — they are an automated first-pass layer. Always combine with appropriate human review for high-stakes workflows.

Section 4

Coding & Development

Private code generation, review, debugging, and documentation. Your proprietary source code stays on your server — never routed through GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or any cloud inference provider. Particularly important for teams working under NDA or with competitive IP.

Section 5

Reasoning & Agentic

Models built for chain-of-thought reasoning, multi-step analysis, tool calling, and agentic workflows. Use these when a general assistant produces plausible-sounding but unauditable answers — and when you need to show your working to a compliance team or auditor.

Section 6

Embeddings & RAG

Embedding models turn documents into searchable vectors for private semantic search and retrieval-augmented generation. These are not chatbots — they are the search layer that a generative model queries. Pair an embedding model with a generative model to build a private knowledge base.

RAG architecture: Embedding model converts your documents to vectors → vector database stores them → user query gets embedded → nearest-neighbour search retrieves relevant chunks → generative model answers with context. All of this can run privately on a single server.
Section 7

Tiny / Edge / Lightweight

Real AI on minimal hardware — Raspberry Pi, 1–2 GB Lightsail instances, air-gapped field devices, embedded applications. These are not cut-down versions of larger models — most are purpose-built or specifically optimized for constrained hardware.

Section 8

Community & Fine-tunes

Fine-tuned variants, specialized tools, legacy models, and niche specialists from the open-source community. Useful for specific workflows, research baselines, and situations where a general model's default behavior isn't quite right. Always verify licensing and Ollama availability before building around these.

Section 9

Vision & Multimodal

Models that process images alongside text. Use these to analyze screenshots, extract data from scanned documents, interpret charts and diagrams, and answer questions about visual content — without sending image data to cloud vision APIs. Most require GPU hardware for practical inference speeds.

Section 10

Multilingual Specialists

Models with genuine depth in languages that English-first families treat as afterthoughts. If your business communicates in Arabic, Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese, Korean, or other non-European languages at scale, these families are worth evaluating alongside or instead of the flagship English-primary models.
