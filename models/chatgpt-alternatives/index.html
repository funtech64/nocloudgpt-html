🃏 Model cards

Each card links to the full guide page. The score shown is MT-Bench unless marked otherwise.

Alpaca 7B
Stanford CRFM · March 2023
Historical
The model that started the open-source instruction-following wave. Fine-tuned Llama on 52K instructions generated for $500 using GPT-3.5 self-instruct. No longer competitive but foundationally important for understanding how the field developed.
4K context 4–5 GB RAM 52K self-instruct
Non-commercial Full guide →
Dolphin 2.x
Eric Hartford · 2023–2024
~7.8
Fine-tuned on the Dolphin dataset to remove alignment guardrails, making it the preferred base for custom agents where the operator implements their own safety layer. Available in Mistral, Mixtral, and Llama 3 variants. Commercial use permitted.
32K context 5–6 GB RAM Uncensored
Apache 2.0 Full guide →
GPT-2
OpenAI · February 2019
Completion
OpenAI's landmark 2019 model, now fully open under MIT. Not a chat assistant — a text completion model. Indispensable as a teaching reference: small enough to study in full, well-documented, and available in four sizes from 124M to 1.5B.
1K context 1 GB RAM (large) 124M–1.5B
MIT Full guide →
Neural Chat 7B
Intel Labs · November 2023
7.98
Intel's fine-tune of Mistral 7B, optimised for Intel CPU and Gaudi accelerator deployment via OpenVINO. Achieved 7.98 MT-Bench — briefly the highest-scoring 7B model. Ideal when deploying on Intel enterprise hardware.
8K context 5–6 GB RAM Intel OpenVINO
Apache 2.0 Full guide →
Nous Hermes 2
Nous Research · 2023–2024
~8.5
Nous Research's flagship fine-tune series, available on Mistral 7B, Mixtral 8x7B, and Yi 34B bases. The Mixtral variant is best for commercial deployments requiring strong reasoning and tool use. All variants are Apache 2.0.
32K context 7B / 47B variants Tool use
Apache 2.0 Full guide →
OpenChat 3.5
Tsinghua THUNLP · Oct 2023
7.81
Uses Conditioned Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning (C-RLFT) to distinguish between GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 quality training data. 7.81 MT-Bench matches ChatGPT 3.5 on this benchmark. Strong first choice for most general-purpose local chat.
8K context 5–6 GB RAM C-RLFT
Apache 2.0 Full guide →
Orca 1
Microsoft Research · June 2023
~7.0
Introduced "explanation tuning" — training on GPT-4's reasoning traces, not just its answers. Demonstrated that small models could learn to reason at much higher quality when given step-by-step explanations during training. Llama 2 base; non-commercial only.
4K context 5–6 GB RAM Explanation tuning
Non-commercial Full guide →
Orca 2
Microsoft Research · Nov 2023
~7.2
Extends Orca's approach by teaching "cautious reasoning" — training models to select appropriate problem-solving strategies (step-by-step, recall, direct answer) rather than defaulting to one approach for all tasks. Llama 2 base; non-commercial only.
4K context 5–6 GB RAM Strategy selection
Non-commercial Full guide →
Starling 7B β
UC Berkeley · November 2023
8.09
UC Berkeley's RLAIF (Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback) model. Trained PairRM on the Nectar dataset — 183K prompts with GPT-4-ranked completions — then fine-tuned OpenChat 3.5 against it. Highest MT-Bench score at the 7B scale at release.
8K context 5–6 GB RAM RLAIF / PairRM
Apache 2.0 Full guide →
Vicuna 1.5
LMSYS Org · July 2023
7.92
Fine-tuned on 70K real ShareGPT conversations — making it unusually naturalistic in multi-turn dialogue compared to synthetically trained alternatives. Llama 2 base. Non-commercial only (CC BY NC 4.0). Foundational LMSYS benchmark model.
4K context 5–6 GB RAM ShareGPT data
Non-commercial Full guide →
Whisper large-v3-turbo
OpenAI · Sept 2022 / Oct 2024
STT model
OpenAI's best speech-to-text model, now MIT licensed. large-v3-turbo (809M params, Oct 2024) delivers large-v3 quality at 3× the speed. Supports 99+ languages with automatic language detection. Runs locally in 6 GB RAM.
99+ languages 6 GB RAM 809M params
MIT Full guide →
WizardLM-2 7B
Microsoft Research · April 2024
8.01
Uses Evol-Instruct to automatically generate increasingly complex training data through 5 rounds of LLM-driven evolution. WizardLM-2 7B (Mistral base, Apache 2.0, 32K context) achieves 8.01 MT-Bench. WizardCoder 34B reaches 73.2% HumanEval.
32K context 5–6 GB RAM Evol-Instruct
Mixed by variant Full guide →
Zephyr 7B β
HuggingFace · October 2023
7.34
HuggingFace's proof that alignment without human labellers works. Uses Direct Preference Optimisation (dDPO) with the AI-generated UltraFeedback dataset. MIT licence — the most permissive in this guide. Reference model for studying modern alignment techniques.
8K context 5–6 GB RAM dDPO / UltraFeedback
MIT Full guide →
