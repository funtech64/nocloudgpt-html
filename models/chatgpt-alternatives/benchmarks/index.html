What the Numbers Actually Mean

📉

Local models are 15–30% behind on raw scores

The gap between GPT-4o and the best local 7–8B models is real and significant. On complex reasoning, multi-step tasks, and creative writing, you will notice the difference. Don't let anyone tell you otherwise.

📈

The gap has halved in 18 months

Llama 3.1 8B scores 73% on MMLU — higher than GPT-3.5 did in 2022. The local model trajectory is steep. Today's "behind" is yesterday's "state of the art."

🔒

Local wins on privacy, cost, and control

Zero data leaves your machine. Zero per-token cost after hardware. No API rate limits, no service outages, no terms-of-service changes that affect your workflow overnight.

🎯

For many real tasks, 7B is good enough

Summarization, classification, light coding, Q&A over documents, chat interfaces — Mistral 7B and Llama 3.1 8B handle these well. You don't need GPT-4o for everything.

⚠️

Coding is the biggest local weakness

HumanEval tells the story: GPT-4o at 90% vs Mistral 7B at 30.5%. For production code generation, complex debugging, or full-stack scaffolding, local 7B models aren't there yet. Use Codestral or Llama 3.3 70B for serious coding work.

🏃

Alpaca is a historical artifact, not a tool

2K context, ~42% MMLU, no safety tuning. Alpaca proved the distillation concept in 2023. In 2025 it belongs in a museum, not a production pipeline.

What These Benchmarks Don't Measure

🕵️ Data Privacy

MMLU doesn't score whether your prompts get logged, trained on, or subpoenaed. For regulated industries, this is the only metric that matters.

🔌 Offline Operation

Benchmarks assume connectivity. Air-gapped environments, field deployments, and low-bandwidth situations require local models regardless of benchmark rank.

💰 Total Cost at Scale

GPT-4o at $0.005/1K tokens looks cheap per query. At 10M queries/month for an internal tool, that's $50K+/month. Local amortizes to near zero.

🛠️ Fine-Tuning Control

You can fine-tune Mistral or Llama on your domain data. You cannot fine-tune GPT-4o. Domain-specific fine-tuning can close the benchmark gap substantially.

⚡ Latency

Cloud API roundtrips add 200–800ms. A local model on decent hardware returns tokens in milliseconds. For real-time applications, local wins on latency.

📜 Compliance

HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2, EU AI Act. Benchmarks ignore compliance. Your legal team doesn't. Local deployment can be the difference between shipping and not shipping.

Benchmark Methodology Notes

MMLU

Massive Multitask Language Understanding. 57 subjects, 4-choice questions. Tests breadth of knowledge. Higher = better general knowledge coverage.

MT-Bench

Multi-turn instruction following benchmark by LMSYS. GPT-4 judges responses on a 1–10 scale. Tests conversational ability and instruction adherence.

HumanEval

OpenAI's Python coding benchmark. 164 problems judged by passing test cases. Direct measure of functional code generation quality.

Quantization Note

Local scores use Q4_K_M quantization (standard for consumer hardware). Full-precision scores are ~2–4% higher but require significantly more VRAM.
