Dolphin

Community Fine-Tuned AI for Open-Context Private Workflows

Dolphin is a series of fine-tuned models from Eric Hartford, built on top of Llama, Mistral, and other base models. Dolphin models reduce the default instruction restrictions of the base models, making them popular for creative writing, research, unrestricted Q&A, and internal workflows where the default guardrails of commercial models interfere with legitimate use cases.

Who developed it

Eric Hartford (Community)

[Eric Hartford (Community) — developer badge]

Dolphin is produced by Eric Hartford, a member of the open-source AI community. The fine-tunes use synthetic instruction data to train models with reduced default refusals and more open context following. Dolphin models are popular in the homelab, creative writing, and research communities.

Why teams choose it

Fast, Efficient Pre-Built Private AI

Reduced default restrictions

For private internal use where the base model's default refusals interfere with legitimate research, creative writing, or technical work — Dolphin's training removes many of those blockers.

Private deployment ensures appropriate access control

When Dolphin runs on your own server with access controls you configure, the reduced default restrictions are scoped to your authorized users — not exposed publicly.

Best-fit workloads

What to Use Dolphin For

Creative and fiction writing

Longer-form fiction, world-building, and creative projects where default model restrictions limit narrative exploration.

Research and analysis

Open-ended research assistance where default refusals prevent legitimate academic or professional exploration of sensitive topics.

Internal tools with custom guardrails

Teams building internal AI tools who want to implement their own policy layer rather than the base model's defaults.

Homelab and developer experimentation

Developers building and testing private AI applications who need a model that follows instructions without excessive refusals.

Honest guidance

When to Choose a Different Model

You need built-in content safety for public-facing AI

Dolphin is designed for private internal use with your own access controls. For public-facing applications, a model with built-in safety layers or an explicit safety classifier is more appropriate. See Llama Guard →
