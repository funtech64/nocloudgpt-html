MiMo

XiaoMi's Open Reasoning Model — Mathematical and Analytical AI on Your Server

MiMo is XiaoMi's open-weight reasoning model, trained with reinforcement learning to improve mathematical reasoning and problem-solving accuracy. It applies techniques similar to DeepSeek R1's training approach to a smaller, more accessible model size — making structured reasoning available on Standard AI hardware.

Who developed it

XiaoMi AI Lab

[XiaoMi AI Lab — developer badge]

MiMo was released by XiaoMi's AI research organization in 2025. XiaoMi is a Chinese consumer electronics and AI company. MiMo-7B applies reinforcement learning post-training to improve mathematical reasoning accuracy in a 7B parameter model, following the approach demonstrated by DeepSeek R1.

Why teams choose it

Reasoning quality at 7B on Standard AI

MiMo targets mathematical reasoning improvement in a 7B model — accessible on Standard AI hardware without GPU infrastructure for teams that need reasoning-focused AI on a budget.

Reinforcement learning trained for accuracy

Post-training with RL to reduce mathematical errors and improve structured reasoning — the same approach that made DeepSeek R1 notable, at a smaller scale.
