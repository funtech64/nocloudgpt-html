License rule

Glass Licenses are interchangeable. Deployment Keys are not. A Glass License is the portable customer capacity unit. A Deployment Key is the revocable credential for one active deployment.

No Jet License

Jet Agents are curated cloud-model lanes inside the package, not a separate license type.

Server capacity

A Glass License can support server/deployment capacity according to the customer plan.

Agent growth

Customers can grow from private hosts into Glass Agents, Jet Agent lanes, and later Alchemist coordination.

Additional Glass Licenses

  • Sunrise Starter additional licenses: $100 each.
  • Starter customers who reach $400 total spend can receive a bulk gift upgrade into the Group Plan structure.
  • Group Plan additional licenses start at $50 each inside the same organization.
  • Each additional Group Plan license drops by $5 until reaching a $25 floor.
