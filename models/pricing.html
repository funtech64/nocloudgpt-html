Starter fit

Lightweight models

  • Small Ollama models and embeddings

Business fit

Reasoning & coding

  • Mid-size and large instruction models
  • Multi-user chat and internal assistants
  • Persistent storage and RAG workflows
  • GPU-assisted inference on owned hardware

Enterprise fit

Heavy private AI

  • Dedicated GPU servers or clusters
  • Proxmox and private cloud layouts
  • Advanced compliance and isolation needs
  • Multiple concurrent model lanes
