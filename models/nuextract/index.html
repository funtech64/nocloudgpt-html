NuExtract — Structured Data Extraction Without Sending Your Documents to the Cloud

NuExtract is NuMind's purpose-built extraction model — give it a document and a JSON schema, and it returns structured data. Deploy it privately and contracts, medical records, financial filings, and internal reports stay on your infrastructure throughout the extraction pipeline.

Structured data extraction — pulling specific fields, entities, relationships, and values from unstructured text into a consistent JSON format — is one of the most common AI tasks in enterprise automation. Invoice processing, contract review, medical record parsing, regulatory filing analysis, and customer data extraction all follow the same pattern: take a document in, get structured data out. The question is how you do it and where the document goes in the process.

NuExtract was built by NuMind specifically for this task. Unlike general-purpose language models prompted to do extraction, NuExtract is trained on extraction tasks: you provide a template schema specifying what fields you want, provide the source document, and the model returns filled JSON. The specialization produces more consistent schema adherence than general models, particularly on longer documents and complex nested schemas. The model is available in 1B, 3.8B, and a larger NuExtract-large variant, making private deployment accessible from very modest hardware up to production server configurations.

The privacy case for private NuExtract deployment is direct. Document extraction workflows typically operate on the most sensitive material an organization handles: contracts with pricing and liability terms, medical records with PHI, financial statements with non-public information, employment documents with personal data, and legal filings with privileged content. Routing this material through an external AI API creates data handling obligations, potential compliance violations, and IP exposure that a private deployment avoids entirely. NuExtract running on your server means your documents are processed locally and never transmitted to a third party.

What NuExtract Was Built to Do

NuExtract's interface is deliberately simple: provide a JSON template with the fields you want extracted (with optional example values to guide format), provide the source text, and receive filled JSON. The model handles the extraction, type inference, and schema adherence. For repeating document types — where you process hundreds or thousands of documents with the same structure — this consistency is the primary value over a general model.

The 1B variant is small enough to run on CPU-only hardware at practical speeds for batch processing workflows. For high-volume pipelines where extraction speed matters more than perfect accuracy on ambiguous fields, NuExtract-1B deployed on a multi-core server can process substantial document volumes without GPU hardware. The 3.8B variant improves accuracy on complex schemas and longer documents while remaining accessible on modest GPU hardware. NuExtract-large handles the most demanding extraction tasks where field complexity, document length, or schema depth push the smaller variants to their limits.

Where NuExtract Consistently Delivers

Contract and legal document extraction

Parties, dates, payment terms, obligations, termination clauses, jurisdiction — NuExtract pulls structured fields from contracts reliably, handling the legal prose patterns that confuse general models. For legal operations teams processing high volumes of standard contract types, this directly reduces manual review time.

Medical record and clinical document parsing

Diagnoses, medications, dosages, dates, provider names, procedure codes — extracting structured data from clinical notes and medical records is a high-value, high-sensitivity task. Private NuExtract deployment means PHI never leaves your HIPAA-compliant environment while still enabling AI-assisted data extraction at scale.

Financial filing and report analysis

Revenue figures, line items, dates, entity names, and financial ratios from earnings reports, 10-K filings, invoices, and purchase orders. For finance teams, private extraction means material non-public information stays internal while still enabling AI-assisted processing at analyst-useful speeds.

Resume and application screening

Skills, experience duration, job titles, education, and contact information extracted consistently across candidate applications — enabling structured comparison and database population from unstructured CV submissions. Private deployment means applicant personal data stays within your HR systems.

Product and catalog data extraction

Technical specifications, dimensions, compatibility information, and pricing from supplier documents, datasheets, and product descriptions — extracted into a consistent schema for catalog management and e-commerce systems. Particularly valuable for organizations managing large numbers of SKUs across multiple suppliers.

Pipeline automation and ETL integration

NuExtract's consistent JSON output makes it directly integrable into ETL pipelines, document management systems, and automation workflows. Unlike a general chatbot where you need to parse natural language output, NuExtract gives you structured data you can feed directly into databases, APIs, and downstream systems.

Who Should Be Running NuExtract

Legal operations and contract management teams

Law firms, in-house legal departments, and contract management operations processing hundreds of contracts per month have a direct business case for private NuExtract deployment. Contract review time is expensive when done manually, and the specific risk of sending contract terms to an external AI service — exposing pricing, liability language, and deal structure to third-party processing — makes cloud-based extraction tools a practical liability. Private NuExtract handles the extraction locally and delivers the structured output the team actually needs.

Healthcare and clinical operations teams

For any organization processing PHI, the question is not whether to use AI-assisted extraction — the productivity case is too strong — but how to do it in a HIPAA-compatible way. Private NuExtract deployment on infrastructure that is already within your compliance boundary answers this question directly. The model processes clinical documents, returns structured data, and never transmits patient information externally.

Financial and accounting operations teams

Invoice processing, accounts payable automation, and financial data extraction are high-volume, repetitive tasks where AI assistance produces immediate cost savings. For public companies or fund managers handling material non-public information, cloud AI services create disclosure risk. Private NuExtract processes financial documents within the organization's own network, and the extracted data is the only thing that moves downstream.

Government and public sector organizations

Government agencies processing public records, benefit applications, regulatory submissions, or any other sensitive constituent data often cannot use commercial cloud AI services due to FedRAMP requirements, data sovereignty mandates, or simply the political and legal risk of routing citizen data through a private technology company's servers. Private NuExtract deployment satisfies these requirements and delivers the extraction capability the organization needs.
