Llama Guard

Private AI Safety and Content Moderation — Inline Guardrails Without External APIs

Llama Guard is Meta's family of content safety classification models designed for inline use in AI pipelines. It screens inputs and outputs against safety taxonomies — flagging harmful content, policy violations, and unsafe AI responses — all running on your own infrastructure without routing sensitive content through external moderation APIs.

The current generation is Llama Guard 3. See the dedicated Llama Guard 3 page for full deployment guidance. VERIFY: Confirm current Ollama tags before deployment.

Who developed it

Meta AI Research

[Meta AI Research — developer badge]

Llama Guard was released by Meta AI Research alongside the Llama 3 family as an open-source safety layer for AI deployments. The current Llama Guard 3 supports a broad taxonomy of safety categories and is designed to work alongside Llama and other generative models as an inline classification layer.

Why teams choose it

Fast, Efficient Pre-Built Private AI

No content leaves your infrastructure

Cloud content moderation services process every flagged input on external servers. Llama Guard running on your server means your users' inputs and your model's outputs are moderated privately.

Inline pipeline integration

Deploy as a pre-inference input filter and post-inference output filter alongside your primary generative model — the dual-layer architecture that robust AI governance requires.
