Who developed it

[Meta AI Research — developer badge]

Llama 3.3 was released by Meta AI Research in December 2024 as a late-generation improvement to the Llama 3 series. The 70B instruct model demonstrated competitive performance with Llama 3.1 405B on several benchmarks while requiring significantly less hardware.
