LFM-2

Liquid Foundation Models — Hybrid Architecture AI for Efficient Long-Context Work

LFM-2 is Liquid AI's second-generation foundation model series, using a hybrid SSM (State Space Model) and attention architecture. This design processes very long contexts more efficiently than pure transformer models, making LFM-2 particularly well-suited for long-document workflows that would be computationally expensive with conventional architectures.

Who developed it

Liquid AI

[Liquid AI — developer badge]

Liquid AI is a research company founded in 2023, focused on alternative AI architectures to the standard transformer. Their LFM (Liquid Foundation Model) series uses a hybrid SSM+attention design developed from their work on liquid neural networks. LFM-2 represents an improvement over their initial release.
