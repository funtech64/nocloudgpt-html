Ministral

Mistral's Compact Edge AI — Function Calling and Long Context on Minimal Hardware

Ministral is Mistral AI's compact model line (3B and 8B), purpose-built for edge deployment, agentic pipelines, and constrained-hardware scenarios. Despite the small footprint, Ministral supports native function calling, 128K context, and Apache 2.0 licensing — making it one of the most capable compact options available for private deployment.

Who developed it

Mistral AI

Ministral was announced by Mistral AI in October 2024 as the "les Ministraux" edge model line. The 8B model in particular achieves state-of-the-art performance for sub-10B models on several benchmarks. Both models carry Apache 2.0 licensing.
