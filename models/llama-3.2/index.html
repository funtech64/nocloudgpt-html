Who this model family is for

Who This Model Family is For

👤 Personal use

Llama 3.2 is a strong choice if you want a capable private AI assistant that runs entirely on your own hardware. The 1B and 3B models are lightweight enough to run comfortably on a modern laptop or mini PC, making them practical for everyday tasks like drafting emails, summarizing notes, and quick research. The 11B vision model adds a genuinely useful capability: you can ask questions about images, screenshots, PDFs, or charts without uploading anything to the cloud. The smaller text models trade some reasoning depth for speed and accessibility, so they work best as helpful assistants rather than deep analytical engines.

🏢 Business / team use

For teams that handle sensitive documents or client data, Llama 3.2 deployed on-premises keeps all conversations inside your infrastructure. The 11B vision model is particularly valuable in business settings where staff need to query charts, scanned documents, invoices, or presentation slides without sending them to external services. The 3B model works well as a lightweight internal assistant for FAQs, templated responses, and first-pass drafting. Organizations in regulated industries (healthcare, legal, finance) will appreciate the combination of usable performance and strong data privacy.

⚙️ Developer & technical use

Llama 3.2 is one of the more accessible model families to self-host. The 1B and 3B models are well-suited for edge deployments, local copilots, and applications where low latency and a small memory footprint matter. The 11B vision model brings multimodal capabilities that are still relatively rare in open models — you can pass images directly into prompts and receive structured, contextual responses. This opens up practical use cases in document processing and visual question answering. Developers integrating via Ollama will find the API behavior consistent and reliable.

🔬 Data science & research

Researchers benefit from having a fixed, locally hosted model that doesn't change behavior between sessions. The 11B and 90B vision variants are particularly relevant for multimodal research tasks such as visual question answering, image captioning, and structured extraction from figures and tables. The smaller 1B and 3B models serve well as lightweight baselines or for high-volume experimentation. Note that the 3B model shows clear limitations on complex multi-step reasoning compared to larger variants — use the 90B when that depth is required.

Licensing information

Licensing Information

Llama 3.2 is released under the Llama 3.2 Community License. This license permits both personal and commercial use for most organizations. The main restriction is that companies with more than 700 million monthly active users must obtain a separate license directly from Meta.

The vision models (11B and 90B) carry the same license terms as the text models in this family. You should review the full license before deploying in a commercial context, especially if you are in a regulated industry or plan to redistribute the model.
