Grok / xAI

xAI's Grok Models — What's Available for Private Deployment

Grok is the AI model series developed by xAI, Elon Musk's AI company. Grok 1 (314B MoE) was released as open-weight in March 2024, but requires exceptional hardware. Grok 2 and later models are closed-source, API-only products. This page covers what is realistically deployable privately and when to consider alternatives.

IMPORTANT: Grok 1 (314B MoE) is the only truly open-weight Grok model. It requires enormous hardware to run. Grok 2+ are closed-source APIs only. For most private AI deployments, alternatives like DeepSeek, Llama 4, or Mixtral are the practical choice.

Who this model family is for

Who This Model Family is For

👤 Personal use

For most individuals, self-hosting Grok is not realistic. The only open-weight version (Grok-1) has 314 billion parameters and requires multiple high-end GPUs just to load. If you are an enthusiast or researcher curious about xAI's architecture and want to study a genuinely massive open-weight model, Grok-1 is intellectually interesting — but for everyday personal use, a smaller, instruction-tuned model will serve you far better.

🏢 Business / team use

Grok-1 is only practical for organizations with significant GPU infrastructure that specifically want a very large, fully transparent base model under their own control. Because the open Grok-1 is a base model (not heavily instruction-tuned like later Grok versions), your team should expect to invest in fine-tuning and prompt engineering before it behaves like the polished conversational experience available through xAI's API. For most teams that simply need a helpful assistant, newer and more efficient open models are usually the better choice.

⚙️ Developer & technical use

Developers who want to inspect, modify, and experiment with a frontier-scale Mixture-of-Experts architecture will find Grok-1 genuinely valuable — it is one of the few truly large open-weight models available. You get full access to the weights and an interesting architecture worth studying. However, building reliable applications on top of it requires handling its base-model behavior, high hardware demands, and the lack of the tool-use and real-time features found only in xAI's closed versions.

🔬 Data science & research

This is where Grok-1 is most useful. Researchers studying large-scale Mixture-of-Experts models, scaling behavior, or working with an unmodified base model at frontier scale get rare, transparent access to a 314B-parameter system. It serves as an excellent reference point for academic work, benchmarking, and fine-tuning experiments — provided your lab or organization has the necessary compute resources.

Licensing information

Licensing Information

Grok-1 is released by xAI under the Apache 2.0 License, which permits commercial use, modification, and redistribution. This is a genuinely permissive license for a model of this scale.

Important limitations to understand:

  • Only Grok-1 is open-weight. Grok-2 and all later versions are closed and available exclusively through xAI's API.
  • Grok-1 is a base model, not a heavily instruction-tuned conversational model. Expect to fine-tune it for production-quality behavior.
  • Practical self-hosting is limited to well-resourced teams due to its massive size.
